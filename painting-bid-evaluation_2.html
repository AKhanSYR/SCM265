Bid Room: Room 204B Repaint

Three vendors have submitted quotes to repaint Room 204B. Your job: evaluate each bid on financial and technical merit, weight the criteria, and decide who wins the contract.

Scenario

Room 204B needs repainting before next term. Procurement issued a Request for Quote (RFQ), and three vendors responded: Premier Painters Inc., BudgetBrush Co., and EcoTone Finishes. No single bid wins on every dimension — that's the point. Your team will run the full supplier-scoring process end to end and decide who gets the work.

01

Set your weights

Before you look at a single bid, open Weight the Criteria and decide as a team how much each criterion should matter. Locking this in first keeps your priorities from being swayed by which vendor looks appealing.

02

Read the bids

Open The Bids tab. Each quote has financial terms and a technical write-up — read all three before scoring anything.

03

Score the financials

In Finance Ledger, score all 4 financial criteria for each vendor, 1 (weak) to 5 (excellent). Think about cash flow and risk, not just the lowest price.

04

Score the technical merit

In Field Report, score all 5 technical criteria for each vendor using the same 1–5 scale, then check The Award to see your weighted result.
